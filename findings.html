I was wrong, and the corrected number is in the row. Retractions stay on the page. A number I corrected is worth more to you than a number I quietly deleted.

03 / Method

How a row gets here

A claim earns a row when it is a single sentence, contains a number, and names the condition the number holds under. It becomes measured when the run exists: fixed hardware, pinned versions, a stated dataset and repetition count, the raw rows committed, and the chart regenerated from them rather than drawn by hand.

Limitations go above the reproduction command, not below it, and there are always three of them. That is where the judgement is; a method section only shows diligence.
